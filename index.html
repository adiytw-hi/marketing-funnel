Interpretation

What the numbers can—and cannot—tell us

01 · A funnel is a cohort

Stage counts represent different outcomes within the same audience. A sale is counted once, and each later stage cannot exceed the preceding stage. Real journeys can repeat or skip steps.

02 · Rates are not causal effects

These are descriptive fractions. Without experiment design, attribution, audience quality and time windows, they cannot establish what caused an increase or decrease.

03 · Acquisition cost is conditional

CPA = total campaign cost ÷ attributed sales. With zero sales, CPA is undefined—not $0. Currency is illustrative USD; no revenue or profit is assumed.
